1. Introduction & Agreement

Welcome to RIZZ EV. By onboarding with us, renting an electric vehicle, or utilizing our services, you agree to strictly follow these Terms and Conditions. Please review all terms carefully before accepting vehicle possession.

2. Member Eligibility & Requirements

To qualify as an eligible rider on the RIZZ EV platform, you must meet the following mandatory conditions:

  • Age Requirement: You must be at least 18 years of age or older.
  • Government Identification: You must possess and submit valid government identification for KYC verification (e.g., Aadhaar card, Driving License, or voter ID).
  • Traffic Compliance: Riders must strictly observe all local traffic regulations, speed limits, helmet laws, and road rules at all times while operating a RIZZ EV vehicle.

3. Vehicle Usage, Modifications & Misuse

Vehicles assigned by RIZZ EV are company property meant for authorized commercial delivery usage:

  • No Modifications: Riders are strictly prohibited from modifying, tampering with, painting, altering wiring, or adding external parts/accessories to the vehicle.
  • Misuse Prevention: Riders must not misuse, overload, sub-lease, or perform stunts with the bike. Misuse will lead to immediate cancellation of your rental agreement.
  • Damage Penalties: If any damage occurs due to rider negligence, accidents, rash driving, or unauthorized tampering, the rider is strictly liable to pay the full penalty cost associated with the repair or part replacement.

4. Onboarding Fees & Fare Adjustments

Financial guidelines governing rider onboarding and rental pricing:

  • Non-Refundable Onboarding Fee: The mandatory onboarding fee paid during sign-up is non-refundable once the rider takes physical possession of the vehicle.
  • Market Fare Adjustments: RIZZ EV reserves the explicit right to adjust daily or weekly rental rates dynamically based on prevailing market conditions, operational inflation, or industry fare increases.

5. Payment Defaults & Vehicle Recovery

Rental payments must be settled according to the agreed schedule:

  • Default & Repossession: If a rider fails or refuses to pay daily/weekly rent on time, RIZZ EV retains full legal authority to remotely immobilize and recover the vehicle.
  • Recovery Penalty & Pending Dues: During recovery operations, RIZZ EV will assess and collect penalties covering vehicle damage, recovery expenses, and all pending unpaid rents from the rider or their deposit balance.

6. Maintenance, Breakdown & Cooperation

In the event of technical issues or vehicle breakdowns:

  • Rider Cooperation: If a vehicle requires repair or routine servicing, the rider agrees to fully cooperate with RIZZ EV technicians and bring the vehicle to the designated hub as instructed.
  • Replacement Vehicle: If available, RIZZ EV will assign the rider an alternative operational bike as quickly as possible to ensure minimal disruption to their delivery work.
