Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

At RIZZ EV, we prioritize the protection of your personal and operational data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, store, and safeguard information provided by delivery riders, fleet investors, and platform visitors.

2. Information We Collect

We gather necessary information to fulfill EV rentals and fleet management operations:

  • Rider Identity & KYC: Full name, phone number, physical address, driving license, and government-issued identification cards.
  • Investor & Financial Data: Bank details, UPI information, onboarding fee receipts, and monthly payout records.
  • Vehicle Telematics & Location Data: Real-time GPS tracking, battery charge logs, vehicle speed, trip distances, and geofencing telemetry recorded during active rentals.
  • Technical Usage: Device models, IP addresses, and session interaction logs when using our web portal.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly utilized for core service operational efficiency:

  • Processing rider onboarding, vehicle allocation, and daily rental collection.
  • Monitoring vehicle health, battery condition, and preventative maintenance schedules.
  • Preventing theft, unauthorized geofence crossings, and misuse through automated remote telematics.
  • Calculating investor earnings and disbursing monthly fleet yields.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell personal data. Information is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with:

  • Payment Processors: PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways for secure rental transactions.
  • Law Enforcement: Authorities in response to stolen vehicle reports, traffic violations, or official legal inquiries.
  • Enterprise Delivery Partners: Verification of rider operational eligibility when requested by delivery platforms.

5. Data Security

All sensitive information, including identity records and telematics data, is stored securely using encryption standards. Access to user records is restricted strictly to authorized operations personnel.
