Vehicle Rental Agreement

Official Commercial EV Rider Agreement | RIZZ EV Fleet

1. Parties & Purpose

This Vehicle Rental Agreement is entered into between RIZZ EV ("Company") and the assigned rider ("Rider"). Taking possession of a RIZZ EV electric vehicle constitutes full legal acceptance of all terms detailed below.

2. Rider Qualification & Eligibility

The Rider explicitly confirms that they fulfill all standard eligibility criteria:

  • The Rider is 18 years of age or older.
  • The Rider has submitted valid, legal government identification (Aadhaar Card or other government-issued ID).
  • The Rider agrees to strictly obey all applicable traffic rules, speed regulations, and local safety standards.

3. Vehicle Care, Modifications & Misuse Policy

The vehicle remains the exclusive property of RIZZ EV at all times. The Rider agrees to the following usage standards:

  • Strict Modification Ban: The Rider shall not modify, alter, customize, open battery compartments, or adjust any electrical/mechanical parts of the vehicle.
  • Misuse Prohibited: Misuse, aggressive off-roading, stunting, overloading, or sub-leasing the vehicle to third parties is strictly prohibited.
  • Damage Liability: If the vehicle suffers damage due to accidents, negligence, rash driving, or tampering, the Rider must pay full damage penalties and repair costs assessed by RIZZ EV.

4. Onboarding Fee & Fare Schedule

Important Note on Onboarding Fees: The onboarding fee paid during sign-up is strictly non-refundable once the Rider has taken possession of the electric bike.
  • Rental Due Dates: Rent must be paid according to the assigned daily/weekly schedule.
  • Market Rate Adjustments: The Company reserves the right to adjust rental rates dynamically in response to market conditions, inflation, or industry operational fare increases.

5. Non-Payment, Recovery & Dues Assessment

In the event of rental payment default:

  • Authority to Recover: If the Rider fails to pay the required rent on time, the Company holds immediate legal authority to immobilize and repossess the vehicle.
  • Settlement of Pending Dues: Upon vehicle recovery, the Company will calculate and collect all outstanding rental dues along with applicable repossession and damage penalties.

6. Maintenance, Repairs & Bike Replacement

To ensure long-term vehicle health and rider safety:

  • Technical Maintenance Cooperation: If the bike requires routine servicing or unexpected technical repairs, the Rider must fully cooperate with Company technicians and present the vehicle at the designated RIZZ EV hub.
  • Replacement Allocation: In the event of a required repair, subject to availability, the Company will assign the Rider an alternate operational bike as quickly as possible to prevent work disruption.

7. Acknowledgment

By signing the physical or digital onboarding form, paying the onboarding fee, or driving a RIZZ EV vehicle off the hub premises, the Rider acknowledges that they have read, understood, and agreed to be bound by every clause of this agreement.
