Eliminating Operational Friction in Last-Mile Mobility

The urban quick-commerce and delivery ecosystem relies on millions of full-time riders. However, traditional internal combustion engine (ICE) scooters burden drivers with volatile fuel overheads, heavy maintenance downtime, and steep debt cycles.

RIZZ EV replaces this fragmented ownership model with an all-inclusive subscription platform. By providing heavy-duty electric scooters coupled with integrated battery-swapping, we turn high daily operational costs into clear driver profits and predictable fleet yields.
