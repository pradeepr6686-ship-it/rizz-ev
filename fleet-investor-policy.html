Fleet Investor Policy

Asset Deployment & Fleet Owner Partnership Framework | RIZZ EV

1. Overview & Partnership Scope

The RIZZ EV Fleet Investor Policy governs the terms between individual asset owners, fleet investors, or institutional partners ("Fleet Investors") and RIZZ EV ("Platform"). Under this framework, investors deploy commercial electric scooters into our operational ecosystem to generate recurring rental yields.

2. Platform Management Responsibilities

RIZZ EV operates as a full-stack fleet management service to maximize asset utilization. The Platform undertakes the following responsibilities:

  • Rider Onboarding & Verification: Complete KYC screening, background checks, and onboarding of gig-economy delivery riders.
  • Daily Rental Collection: Managing daily rental billing, automated collection systems, and default mitigations.
  • Hub Maintenance & Servicing: Conducting preventive health checks, battery swap hub logistics, and routine component maintenance.
  • IoT Telematics Monitoring: Real-time GPS tracking, geofencing enforcement, and remote vehicle security operations.

3. Yield Disbursal & Revenue Share

Payout Schedule: Rental yields are calculated monthly based on fleet uptime and active rental days, disbursed directly to the investor's designated bank account within the first 7 business days of each calendar month.
  • Management Fee: The Platform retains a fixed percentage of gross rental collections as an operational management fee covering rider management, hub operations, and software infrastructure.
  • Financial Transparency: Fleet Investors receive monthly operational reports covering mileage, battery health logs, uptime percentages, and itemized net yields.

4. Asset Protection & Insurance

Protecting the capital investment of our fleet partners is a core priority:

  • Commercial Insurance: All deployed vehicles must maintain valid commercial third-party and comprehensive insurance coverage.
  • Theft & Repossession Protocols: Vehicles are equipped with remote IoT cut-offs. In the event of rider default, misuse, or suspected theft, RIZZ EV initiates immediate automated immobilization and field recovery operations.
  • Damage Cost Recovery: Damages caused by rider negligence are covered via rider onboarding deposits or billed directly to the defaulting rider.

5. Contract Term & Asset Exit Strategy

Flexibility and clear exit parameters for fleet partners:

  • Minimum Tenure: Vehicles deployed into the RIZZ EV network are subject to a minimum lock-in period specified in the individual Investor Agreement (typically 6 to 12 months).
  • Asset Withdrawal: Fleet Investors wishing to withdraw assets upon agreement maturity must provide a 30-day written notice to facilitate smooth rider re-assignment.
