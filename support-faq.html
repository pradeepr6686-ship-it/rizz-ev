Rider Onboarding & Rentals
You can apply online via our Rider Portal or visit any RIZZ EV Hub. Submit your government photo ID, pay the initial onboarding fee, complete a quick verification check, and pick up your scooter on the same day.
You need a valid government-issued ID (such as an Aadhaar Card or Voter ID) along with your current address proof and basic contact details.
As outlined in our Rental Agreement, the onboarding fee covers administrative registration, background checks, and initial vehicle allocation, and is non-refundable once vehicle possession is taken.
Payments & Daily Fares
Daily rental fees are automatically collected via digital UPI/Debit channels according to your assigned schedule. You can manage and track payments directly through your RIZZ EV Rider Dashboard.
Failure to pay rental dues on time may result in remote vehicle immobilization via our IoT network. Outstanding balances must be cleared along with applicable penalty charges before vehicle access is restored.
Maintenance & Battery Swapping
You can visit any designated RIZZ EV Swap Hub across the city to replace low batteries with fully charged units in under 2 minutes.
Immediately pull over safely and contact our 24/7 Helpline at +91 93191 16117. Do not attempt unauthorized repairs or modifications. Our field service team will assist with recovery or issue a replacement bike subject to availability.
Fleet Investors & Asset Partners
Fleet owners and individual investors can register on our Fleet Owners page or reach out to investor@rizzev.com. We onboard, manage rider allocations, oversee servicing, and disburse monthly returns to asset owners.
